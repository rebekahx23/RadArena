# Findings and Impression

Reporting principle: describe observations in Findings and communicate the conclusion
in Impression. A reporting template can improve organization without supplying facts
about a particular study. Use appropriate terminology and acknowledge relevant limitations.

For the RadArena task, preserve the supplied sentence IDs. When the same finding recurs,
check that its presence, location, side, and severity are consistent. Distinguish repeated
mentions from separate abnormalities. Do not manufacture technique, history, or prior
imaging information to complete a template. These last rules are project-authored.

Sources: [RSNA RadReport](https://www.rsna.org/practice-tools/data-tools-and-standards/radreport-reporting-templates),
