# Claim attributes and terminology

Project-authored representation: finding, presence/absence, uncertainty, anatomy,
laterality, severity, and sentence ID. Track these separately; matching finding words
does not guarantee the whole claim matches.

Fictional wording example: "possible pleural effusion" expresses uncertainty and is
not equivalent to "no pleural effusion". Preserve hedging unless supplied evidence
supports resolving it. Preserve qualifiers such as mild/moderate/severe rather than
inventing quantitative cutoffs. Validate laterality using available orientation evidence.

Use consistent terminology. [RSNA RadLex](https://www.rsna.org/practice-tools/data-tools-and-standards/radlex-radiology-lexicon)
is a terminology resource; an ontology export and validated synonym mapping are not
included here. The attribute schema and example above are project rules, not RadLex definitions.
