# Cardiac enlargement

Source-grounded background: a chest radiograph can suggest an enlarged heart, while
additional testing may be needed to establish whether enlargement is real and determine
its cause. A descriptive size finding alone does not specify cardiac function or etiology.

Additional source-grounded background: Eze describes cardiothoracic ratio as transverse
cardiac width relative to internal thoracic width on a standard PA radiograph. The book
also describes AP magnification as a limitation on cardiac-size assessment. Do not turn
an apparently large silhouette into a precise size measurement or severity label when
projection or measurement support is missing.

No validated cardiac-size measurement procedure or severity thresholds are included.
