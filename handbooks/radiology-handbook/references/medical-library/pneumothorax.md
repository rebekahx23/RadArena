# Pneumothorax

Source-grounded background: pneumothorax involves air in the pleural space. Chest imaging
is among the tests used to investigate air or fluid in that space. The name alone does
not establish extent, side, cause, or clinical urgency in a particular case.

The background distinction here is air in the pleural space rather than excess fluid.
This chapter supplies no validated image-sign checklist, size threshold, or clinical
management recommendation. Radiologist review is pending. Operational claim-checking
steps belong to the active role's workflow chapter.
