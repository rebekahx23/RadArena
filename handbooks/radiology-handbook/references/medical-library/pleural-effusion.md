# Pleural effusion

Source-grounded background: pleural effusion means excess fluid in the pleural space
between the lung covering and chest-wall lining. The definition does not establish a
cause, side, amount, or visibility on the supplied image.

The definition supplies background rather than radiographic detection criteria. It
does not assign mild/moderate/severe categories or distinguish a cause from an image.
No numerical severity thresholds or sensitivity claims are provided. Radiologist review
