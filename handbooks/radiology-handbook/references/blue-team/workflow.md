# Blue verification workflow

Project-authored operational guidance; review pending.

Identify candidate claims and their presence, certainty, anatomy, side, and severity.
Plan neutral image questions rather than asking the tool to confirm a presumed answer.
For a unilateral claim, checking existence alone is insufficient to resolve its side.
For severity, describe the evidence and limitations instead of assuming the report's
qualifier is correct. Preserve uncertainty when the available views cannot decide.

The current controller permits at most three focused image checks. Use observations
to identify contradicted sentences and cite the actual observation IDs. References
may clarify how to assess a claim but do not establish the current image's findings.

Keep verdicts distinct: error, no error detected, uncertain. A report with an unsupported
or unresolved claim is not automatically a proven erroneous report. Likewise, fluent
wording does not establish image support.
