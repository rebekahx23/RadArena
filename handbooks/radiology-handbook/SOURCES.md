# Sources and provenance

The handbook contains original RadArena text and short, cited summaries of public
sources and selected passages from a supplied reference book. It contains no imported
clinical cases, held-out report examples, full textbook extracts, or textbook images.
Qualified radiologist review is pending.

Reporting sources: RSNA RadReport and RadLex overviews, linked in the reporting
chapters. The ACR parameter was available through official search excerpts only; the
full-document fetch was blocked. Medical sources: NCI's pleural-effusion dictionary
entry, NHLBI's pleural-disorder pages, and Mayo Clinic's enlarged-heart diagnostic
overview. Chapter links identify the sources. Those descriptions do not establish
endorsement of this handbook.

Book source: Kenneth C. Eze. *Radiology for Medical Students*. Mindex Publishing Co.
Ltd., Benin City, Nigeria, 2012. ISBN 978-978-8448-41-9. The user-supplied 230-page PDF
is local and excluded from Git. `knowledge/README.md` records its SHA-256 and page map.
Selected original summaries cover technique (printed pp. 1-2 / PDF pp. 15-16),
systematic review (Table 1.2, printed p. 5 / PDF p. 19), and cardiac-size assessment
(printed pp. 39-41 / PDF pp. 53-55). No numerical thresholds, population-specific
cutoffs, management recommendations, or book cases have been imported. This is a 2012
source; the selected notes require clinical review and are not current guideline claims.

The workflow chapters, claim schema, error families, tool-status descriptions, and
verification questions are project-authored. The medical chapters separate source-backed
background from these questions. Do not represent the questions as clinically validated
detection algorithms. Recheck references and obtain clinical review before expanding the
medical appendix or making accuracy claims.

Additional supplied background reference: J. S. Benseler, *The Radiology Handbook: A
Pocket Guide to Medical Imaging*, Ohio University Press, 2006, ISBN 978-0-8214-1708-9.
[Publisher record](https://www.ohioswallow.com/9780821417089/the-radiology-handbook/). The local reference library includes this book, but these chapters
have no recorded Benseler page extraction. The implementation's book-derived summaries
are attributed to Eze above. Neither full PDF is redistributed.
